# Shepherd-AI Week 9 Bibliography

This bibliography is organized from the repository literature-review export.
Titles, author lists, years, and venue descriptions are limited to what that
export states. It is a working bibliography, not a venue-formatted final
reference list. Several supplied documents are preprints, and the repository
does not state complete volume, page, DOI, or publisher metadata for every
entry.

## Multi-Drone Language Coordination

**[L1]** Alessandro Nazzari, Roberto Rubinacci, and Marco Lovera. "TACOS:
Task Agnostic Coordinator of a Multi-Drone System." *Drones*, volume 10,
article 25, 2026.

**[L2]** Alejandro Jarabo-Penas, Juan Bravo-Arrabal, Edouard G. A. Rolland,
and Anders Lyhne Christensen. "Swarm-Steward: Scalable and Reliable
Natural-Language Coordination of Autonomous Aerial." IEEE conference paper,
2026. Further venue details are not stated in the supplied review.

**[L3]** Andrea Iannoli, Lorenzo Gigli, Luca Sciullo, Angelo Trotta, and Marco
Di Felice. "Say the Mission, Execute the Swarm: Agent-Enhanced LLM Reasoning
in the Web-of-Drones." Accepted at IEEE WoWMoM 2026, 2026.

**[L11]** Aditya Shibu, Marah Saleh, Mohamed Al-Musleh, and Nidhal Abdulaziz.
"SkySim: A ROS2-based Simulation for Natural Language Control of Drone
Swarms." Second International Conference on Drones and Unmanned Systems,
2026.
