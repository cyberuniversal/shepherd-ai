# Week 4 Research Deferrals

The current Week 4 slice intentionally stops at high-level mission planning. The literature review supports bounded plans and deterministic validation before execution, so this document records what must not be claimed as completed by the Week 4 planner.

Deferred for Week 4:

- Multi-drone scheduling and allocation. The roadmap assigns this to Week 5.
- Route feasibility and route optimization. The current planner emits task sequences and dependency graphs only.
- Safety validation. Battery, altitude, restricted-area, collision, and availability checks are not implemented here.
- Simulation execution. The planner writes artifacts for later scheduling and execution, not executed missions.
